How to scan a document to PDF

  1. Start the camera: tap “Start camera” and allow access when your browser asks.
  2. Capture each page: hold the camera straight above the page and tap the round button. Repeat for every page.
  3. Clean it up: pick “Black & white” for a real scanner look, reorder or rotate pages if needed.
  4. Create the PDF: tap “Create PDF” and download your file.

Tips for a clean scan

  • Use bright, even light and avoid shadows from your hand or phone.
  • Place the page on a dark, flat surface so the edges stand out.
  • Hold the camera parallel to the page, straight above it.

Frequently asked questions

Does the camera work in any browser?

The camera works in modern browsers on a secure (https) page. The first time, your browser asks permission to use the camera. If it is blocked, you can still add photos from your gallery.

Are my scans uploaded?

No. The photos and the PDF are made inside your browser and never leave your device.

How do I get a clean black-and-white scan?

Choose the “Black & white” filter. It removes shadows and makes the page look like a real scanner copy. Use good light and hold the camera straight above the page.

Can I scan many pages into one PDF?

Yes. Capture each page one after another. They are all added to a single PDF in the order you arrange them.
