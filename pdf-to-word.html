How to convert PDF to Word

  1. Select your PDF: click the box above and choose a file.
  2. Convert: click “Convert to Word” and check the preview.
  3. Download: save the .docx file and open it in Word, Google Docs or WPS Office.

What is kept

  • All text, joined into proper paragraphs so it is easy to edit.
  • Headings, based on the size of the text.
  • Page breaks and the original page size.

Images, colors and complex designs are not copied. Scanned PDFs are pictures of text, so they cannot be converted.

Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. The PDF is read inside your browser and the Word file is created on your device.

Will the layout look exactly the same?

The text, paragraphs, headings and page breaks are kept so you can edit easily. Images and complex designs are not copied.

Why is my Word file empty?

The PDF is probably a scanned image. This tool reads PDFs with selectable text.

Does it open in Google Docs?

Yes. The .docx file opens in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, LibreOffice and WPS Office.
