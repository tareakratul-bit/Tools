How to compress images

  1. Select images: choose one or more JPG, PNG or WEBP files.
  2. Pick settings: set the quality, and reduce the width for big phone photos.
  3. Compress: click “Compress images”, then download each file or all of them as a ZIP.

Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded?

No. Images are compressed inside your browser and never leave your device.

What quality should I choose?

75% is a good balance for most photos. The file gets much smaller and the difference is hard to see.

Why did my PNG barely shrink?

PNG keeps every pixel exactly, so quality has no effect. Choose WEBP or JPG as the output format, or reduce the width, to make it much smaller.
