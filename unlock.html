    How to unlock many bank statements at once

    1. Add your files: select several locked PDF statements together, or one Excel/Word file.
    2. Type the password: if the files share one password, type it once. For a file with a different password, a box appears on that file.
    3. Choose the result: one merged PDF, separate files in a ZIP, or convert straight to Excel, Word or images.
    4. Download: the new files open without asking for a password.

    What this tool removes

    • Open password of PDF, Excel and Word files, when you enter it.
    • PDF restrictions that block printing, copying text or editing.
    • Excel “Protect Sheet” and “Protect Workbook” locks, and Word “Restrict Editing”.

    This tool does not guess or crack passwords. If you forgot the password of a bank statement, ask your bank to send it again.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I unlock many files at once?

    Yes. Add several locked PDFs together. If they share one password, type it once. You can then save them as one merged PDF or as separate files in a ZIP.

    Where do I find the password of my bank statement?

    Banks explain it in the email that comes with the statement. It is often a mix of your date of birth, part of your account number or your mobile number.

    Can this tool find a password I don't know?

    No. You need the password to remove it. The tool removes the lock so you don't have to type it again every time you open the file.

    Are my files uploaded?

    No. The files and the password stay in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.
