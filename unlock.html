    How to remove a password from a bank statement

    1. Select the file: your PDF statement, or a locked Excel or Word file.
    2. Type the password: the same one you use to open the file.
    3. Choose the result: an unlocked PDF, or convert it straight to Excel, Word, JPG or PNG.
    4. Download: the new file opens without asking for a password.

    What this tool removes

    • Open password of PDF, Excel and Word files, when you enter it.
    • PDF restrictions that block printing, copying text or editing.
    • Excel “Protect Sheet” and “Protect Workbook” locks, so you can edit cells again.
    • Word editing restrictions set with “Restrict Editing”.

    This tool does not guess or crack passwords. If you forgot the password of a bank statement, ask your bank to send it again.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where do I find the password of my bank statement?

    Banks explain it in the email that comes with the statement. It is often a mix of your date of birth, part of your account number or your mobile number.

    Can this tool find a password I don't know?

    No. You need the password to remove it. The tool removes the lock so you don't have to type it again every time you open the file.

    Are my files uploaded?

    No. The file and the password stay in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

    What locks can be removed?

    The open password of PDF, Excel and Word files, the print, copy and edit restrictions of PDFs, and Protect Sheet and Protect Workbook locks in Excel.
