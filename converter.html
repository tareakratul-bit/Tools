How to use the converter

  1. Choose the formats: pick your file type in “Convert from” and the result in “Convert to”.
  2. Add your file: click the box or drop the file onto it.
  3. Convert and download: adjust the options if you like, click “Convert”, then download.

Frequently asked questions

Which conversions are supported?

PDF to Word, Excel, JPG and PNG. Images (JPG, PNG, WEBP) to PDF, Word, JPG, PNG and WEBP.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Every conversion runs inside your browser, so your files stay on your device.

Can it read Bangla text from images?

Yes. Choose Image to Word and set the text language to Bangla or English + Bangla.
