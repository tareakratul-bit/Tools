How to convert PDF to Excel

  1. Select your PDF: click the box above and choose a file.
  2. Choose options: keep all pages in one sheet or put each page on its own sheet.
  3. Convert: click “Convert to Excel”, check the preview, then download the .xlsx file.

Best results

  • Works best with PDFs created by software: bank statements, invoices, price lists and reports.
  • Scanned PDFs are pictures of text, so there is nothing to read. Use a PDF with selectable text.
  • Complex layouts with merged cells may need small fixes in Excel afterwards.

Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. The PDF is read inside your browser and the Excel file is created on your device.

Why is my Excel file empty?

The PDF is probably a scanned image. This tool reads PDFs with selectable text, such as bank statements, invoices and reports exported from software.

Are numbers kept as numbers?

Yes. Values like 1,250.50 or (300) become real numbers in Excel, so you can add them up right away.

Can I get each page on a separate sheet?

Yes. Choose “One sheet per page” before converting.
